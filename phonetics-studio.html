Consonant Endings

Sort regular past verbs (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/) and plural nouns.

Phonetic Sorter Module

Here we will build a drag-and-drop or click-to-sort exercise. Words like "wanted", "called", and "worked" will appear, and students must classify them into the correct phonetic sound box.

Connected Speech

Understand real-world native English rhythms and linking.

Contextual Listening Room

This module will feature audio/video clips of fast, native speech. Students will practice identifying assimilation, elision, and linking words together.
