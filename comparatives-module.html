Comparatives & Superlatives

Usamos los comparativos para contrastar dos cosas (usando THAN). Usamos los superlativos para destacar uno en un grupo (usando THE).

Tipo de Adjetivo Comparativo (+ than) Superlativo (the +)
Cortos (1 sílaba)
fast, old, tall		 -er than
faster than		 the -est
the fastest
Terminados en -E
nice, large		 -r than
nicer than		 the -st
the nicest
Consonante-Vocal-Consonante
big, hot		 Doble consonante + -er
bigger than		 Doble consonante + -est
the biggest
Terminados en -Y (2 sílabas)
easy, funny		 -ier than
easier than		 the -iest
the easiest
Largos (2+ sílabas)
expensive, modern		 more ... than
more expensive than		 the most ...
the most expensive

Los 3 Irregulares Indispensables

Adjetivo Comparativo Superlativo
good (bueno) better than the best
bad (malo) worse than the worst
far (lejos) farther / further than the farthest / furthest

Igualdad y Desigualdad

Cuando dos cosas son equivalentes, no usamos -er ni more. Usamos el adjetivo en su forma base.

Igualdad (=)

Fórmula: as + adjetivo base + as

Ejemplo: "My brother is as tall as me." (Es tan alto como yo).

Desigualdad (≠)

Fórmula: not as + adjetivo base + as

Ejemplo: "My town is not as noisy as the capital." (No es tan ruidoso como la capital).

⚠️ Los 3 Errores Fatales a evitar

  • ❌ He is as taller as me. → ✅ He is as tall as me. (Nunca lleva -er entre los "as").
  • ❌ My city is more quiet than... → ✅ My city is quieter than... (Adjetivos cortos llevan -er).
  • ❌ This hotel is not as better as... → ✅ This hotel is not as good as... (Volvemos a la forma base).
